# Gravity Falls: Weirdmageddon — The Last Cipher

A choose-your-own-adventure fan fiction set during Gravity Falls: Weirdmageddon.

**This is fan fiction. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Disney, Disney Television Animation, or the creators of Gravity Falls.**

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. No installation required.

## About

You play as Dipper and Mabel Pines, three days into Weirdmageddon. Bill Cipher has won. The sky is broken. The Fearamid looms. You choose what to do next.

Four paths branch from the opening:

- **The Bunker** — Flee underground to Ford's secret research station
- **The Resistance** — Rally the town and fight back from the Mystery Shack
- **The Fearamid** — Go straight to Bill's fortress
- **The Axolotl** — Search for the cosmic being that even Bill fears

There are ~25 endings. Most of them are bad. Good luck finding the ones that aren't.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies. Themed after Journal 3 from the show.
